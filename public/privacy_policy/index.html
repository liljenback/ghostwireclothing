Ghostwire Clothing ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Ghostwire Clothing website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our site.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Analytics Telemetry: Usage patterns, performance metrics, and diagnostic data collected through cookies and similar technologies to help us improve the site.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content.
  • Order Information: When you make a purchase, we collect details necessary to process your order such as shipping address and payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Improve our website's functionality and user experience
  • Identify and fix technical issues
  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Analyze site traffic and usage trends

3. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and analytics tools to collect telemetry data about how visitors interact with our site. This data is used solely to improve your experience and is not sold to third parties.

When you first visit our site, you will be presented with a consent prompt asking whether you agree to analytics data collection. You can change your preference at any time by clicking the Cookie Preferences link in the footer of any page.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as Adobe Experience Platform) to process analytics data on our behalf. These services are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only as instructed by us.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers who assist in operating our website and processing orders, subject to confidentiality agreements.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of analytics data collection at any time via the Cookie Preferences link in the footer
  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data

To exercise these rights, please contact us at ghostwireclothing@gmail.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ghostwireclothing@gmail.com.
